\chapter{Where the derivation stands}\label{ch:summary}

\section{In plain words}

Starting from nothing but $\tau$, $\delta$, $I$ and the statements of the philosophy, the mathematics has
produced: the geometry of relativity in one space and one time dimension; collisions obeying
$E^2=p^2+m^2$; thermal equilibrium; births by crossover with exact halving; a thermostat that keeps the
universe partly in motion; horizon-like giants that take in what meets them and let nothing out; binding;
structures that persist while their members turn over; and a dimension that depends on how far a structure
can reach, coming out near 4 at the horizon. What it has not produced is anything quantum (no circular
phases, so no interference with cancellation), any of the gauge groups of known forces, ordinary
three-dimensional locality, or any of nature's numbers.

\section{Choices that remain}

\begin{enumerate}
  \item $f$ is the product; $\psi$ is built from the two views (Proposition~\ref{prop:eta}).
  \item The combining rule is continuous, symmetric and associative (Theorem~\ref{thm:additivity}).
  \item Timelike pairs are decided one way by the containing part (Chapter~\ref{ch:causal}).
  \item The locality rules: children relate to both parents; an absorbed part's relations and records pass
  to its absorber.
  \item The horizon threshold: the median neighbour difference (Chapter~\ref{ch:horizon}).
\end{enumerate}
The values $s=1$ and $c=\half$, the two-view event, and the activity rule are derived, not chosen.

\section{Open problems}

\begin{enumerate}
  \item \textbf{Circular phases.} Nothing quantum appears: the primitives give hyperbolic phases. The only
  hint is the $\pm i$ produced by latency in the two-part universe (Proposition~\ref{prop:delay2}).
  \item \textbf{Gauge groups.} Records give the boost group; known forces need U(1), SU(2), SU(3).
  \item \textbf{Three-dimensional locality.} The network is small-world; its dimension depends on reach.
  Whether the horizon dimension settles (and where) needs larger universes.
  \item \textbf{The second kind of light.} Residues split into pure difference and pure continuity; the
  philosophy names only the first.
  \item \textbf{Light and Axiom~1.} Light sits at an exact null direction.
  \item \textbf{The budget.} The derived life sum is $2\eta_0$; the working notes say $I$.
  \item \textbf{Strict clocks.} One encounter per tick for both parties has not been tested.
  \item \textbf{Identity of structures.} Structure persistence should be checked against a random baseline.
  \item \textbf{Numbers.} No mass, coupling or ratio of nature has been produced.
\end{enumerate}

\section{Distance to known physics}

A grand unified theory joins electromagnetism, the weak and the strong force in one quantum gauge
symmetry; a theory of everything adds gravity. Measured against that, this work has the kinematic
foundation (relativity-like geometry, energy and momentum, thermodynamics) and several structural echoes
(horizon-like giants, binding with mass defect, a parity-breaking rule, gauge links, a dimension near 4),
but none of the defining ingredients: quantum amplitudes, compact gauge groups, a particle spectrum with
charges and spin, three-dimensional locality, or coupling strengths. Each is a foundational step, not a
refinement, and the first of them, circular phases, is a prerequisite for the rest.
